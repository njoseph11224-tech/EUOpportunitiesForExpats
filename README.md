# 🇪🇺 EU Visa Jobs (EUVisaJobs.eu)

> **The Premier European Visa Sponsorship & Relocation Job Portal**

---

## 🌟 Overview

**EUVisaJobs.eu** is a specialized job portal designed to help international skilled professionals discover job opportunities across Europe that provide official **Work Visa Sponsorship**, **Relocation Grants**, and **Tax Benefits** (such as the German EU Blue Card, Dutch IND 30% Tax Ruling, Irish Critical Skills Employment Permit, and French Passeport Talent).

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Verified Expat Vacancies**: Aggregates job postings offering confirmed visa sponsorship for non-EU applicants across 15+ European countries.
- **Official Government & Company Sources**: Integrates listings from official European labor exchanges (*EURES*, *Make it in Germany*, *UWV Werk.nl*, *Workindenmark*, *Arbetsförmedlingen*, *JobsIreland*, *France Travail*) and top visa-sponsoring employers (*ASML*, *Booking.com*, *SAP*, *Siemens*, *Spotify*, *Novo Nordisk*, *Google*, *Stripe*, *Qonto*).
- **Direct Recruiter Contact**: Provides verified recruiter emails, company emails, and recruiter LinkedIn profiles for direct outreach.
- **Smart Filtering**: Filter jobs by Country, Job Category, Contract Type, and Date Posted.
- **Dedicated Job Pages**: Standalone deep-link pages (`/jobs/[id]`) for sharing and viewing detailed role requirements, visa breakdown, and direct application links.
